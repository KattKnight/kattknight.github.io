Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

This page explains what information Katt Editing collects and what happens to it. Katt Editing is run by Ayan, an independent video editor based in India. Contact: katt.editing@gmail.com.

What this website collects

This website has no sign-up, no cookies, no analytics and no ads. It does not track you.

Fonts, videos and images are served from this website itself, so no other company is contacted when you browse it. The hosting provider that serves the website keeps standard server logs (your IP address, browser type and the page you requested).

The contact form

When you send the form, your name, your email or Instagram/X handle, the link you add (if any) and your answers about the job (what you need, how much, deadline, budget) go to Web3Forms, a form service that forwards them to my Gmail inbox. Web3Forms only receives what you type into the form, plus the technical details every web request carries (your IP address and browser type), and only when you press Send. See Web3Forms' privacy policy. If you'd rather skip it, email me directly.

What you send me when we work together

  • Your email address or social handle, and your messages, so I can reply and send quotes and finished clips.
  • Your footage and links, so I can edit them.
  • Payment details, which are handled by the payment service you pay through (for example PayPal or Wise). I only see your name, the amount and the transaction reference.

How I use it

Only to do the work you asked for, send invoices, and keep the business records Indian tax law requires. I don't sell or share your information, and I don't add you to a mailing list.

Where it is stored

Email and files are kept in a Google account (Gmail and Google Drive) under katt.editing@gmail.com. Editing happens on my own computer.

How long I keep it

  • Raw footage and project files: deleted 30 days after the final delivery, unless you ask me to keep them longer.
  • Finished clips: kept until you ask me to delete them, so I can resend them if needed.
  • Invoices and payment records: kept for as long as Indian tax rules require.

Showing your clips in my portfolio

I only add a client's clips to this portfolio after the client says yes in writing. You can ask me to take them down at any time.

Your choices

Email katt.editing@gmail.com to ask what I hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. I reply within 7 days. If you are in the EU or UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date at the top.
